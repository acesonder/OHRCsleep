Success Stories

Kitchener, ON

A Better Tent City

Community-led tiny shelter village providing dignified housing for 50+ residents with wraparound supports. Started in 2020, now a model for other communities.

  • Individual sleeping cabins
  • Shared washrooms and kitchen
  • On-site case management
  • Peer support program
Learn More →
Kingston, ON

Temporary Zoning for Shelters

Fast-tracked zoning approvals for emergency shelters in commercial zones. Reduced approval time from 6+ months to 30 days.

  • As-of-right permissions
  • Waived parking requirements
  • Streamlined approval process
Learn More →
Toronto, ON

Safe Parking Program

Designated lots for people living in vehicles with security, washrooms, and case management. Reduced dangerous street parking situations.

  • 24/7 security
  • Portable washrooms
  • Outreach workers
  • Connection to housing

What We're Asking For

Specific, actionable requests for Northumberland County and Cobourg Town Council based on OHRC guidance and proven approaches.

Urgent Immediate Actions (0-3 months)

1

Emergency Temporary Use Bylaw

Designate 2-3 sites for legal overnight sleeping with basic facilities.

  • 1-year duration (renewable)
  • Washrooms, water, garbage collection
  • No enforcement between 10pm-7am
Low Cost 30-day implementation
2

Sleep Deprivation Moratorium

Pause nighttime displacement by bylaw officers and police.

  • Align with OHRC guidance on human rights
  • Training for enforcement on rights obligations
  • Clear protocols for daytime outreach
No Cost Immediate
3

Fast-Track Shelter Zoning

Enable quick approval for emergency shelters.

  • As-of-right in commercial/industrial zones
  • Waive parking requirements
  • 30-day approval process
Low Cost Policy change

3-12 months Short-Term Actions

4

Tiny Shelter Village Pilot

20-30 small individual units on municipal land.

  • Temporary use bylaw (3 years)
  • Wraparound support services
  • Model: Kitchener's A Better Tent City
Medium Cost 6 months to launch
5

Safe Parking Program

Designated lots for people living in vehicles.

  • Security and case management
  • Washrooms and garbage
  • Zoning amendment to allow
Low Cost 3 months
6

Harm Reduction Service Zoning

Prepare for safe consumption sites.

  • Pre-approve zoning before provincial approval
  • Reduce delays when sites are approved
  • Multiple potential locations identified
No Cost Policy change

1-2 years Medium-Term Actions

7

Inclusionary Zoning Update

Require affordable units in new developments.

  • Bonusing for supportive housing
  • Diverse housing forms permitted
  • Rooming houses, co-housing allowed
Policy change
8

Surplus Land Strategy

Use municipal/county land for housing.

  • Inventory suitable properties
  • Streamlined approval for non-profits
  • Long-term leases at nominal cost
Asset-based

Speak at Council

Ready-to-use resources to help you make your voice heard at municipal meetings.

3-Minute Speech Template

A proven structure for effective council delegations:

Opening (30 sec)

"Good evening. My name is [Your Name]. I'm here representing [X people] who contributed stories to our community platform."

The Problem (60 sec)

"Our data shows 217 incidents of people being displaced while trying to sleep last month. The average person got 3.2 hours of uninterrupted sleep per night."

OHRC Context (30 sec)

"The Ontario Human Rights Commission is clear: municipalities must ensure people have legal places to exist and rest. Currently, there is nowhere in Cobourg where someone without housing can legally sleep."

The Ask (60 sec)

"We're proposing three temporary zoning changes for 12 months:
1. Designate [specific location] for overnight sleeping with basic services
2. Fast-track emergency shelter approvals in commercial zones
3. Pilot a 20-unit tiny shelter village on [municipal land]"

Close (30 sec)

"These are evidence-based, temporary measures. Kingston, Peterborough, and Toronto have all implemented similar approaches. The cost of inaction is measured in lives lost. Thank you."

Implementation Roadmap

Our phased approach to creating lasting change in Northumberland County.

Phase 1 Months 1-2

Foundation

  • ✅ Consultation with people with lived experience
  • ✅ Platform design and development
  • 🔄 Zoning research and precedent analysis
  • ⏳ Identify 3 potential temporary sites
  • ⏳ Legal review (OHRC compliance)
Deliverable: Platform MVP, initial advocacy brief
Phase 2 Months 3-4

Launch

  • ⏳ Beta testing with service providers
  • ⏳ Story collection begins
  • ⏳ Resource directory populated
  • ⏳ Admin and outreach training
Deliverable: Public launch, first 50 stories
Phase 3 Months 4-6

Advocacy Push

  • ⏳ Present data to council
  • ⏳ Delegation coordination
  • ⏳ Media campaign
  • ⏳ Petition for temporary zoning
  • ⏳ Community town halls
Deliverable: Council motion for temporary measures
Phase 4 Months 6-12

Expansion

  • ⏳ Advanced features based on feedback
  • ⏳ Expand to neighboring municipalities
  • ⏳ Research partnerships
  • ⏳ Sustainability planning
Deliverable: Sustainable platform, replicable toolkit
